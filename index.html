Terms & Conditions

Renters must be 21+ with a valid driving licence and government ID. A refundable security deposit applies to every booking. Service is available only in Bangalore & Odisha. Full details below.

Renters must be at least 21 years old and hold a valid driving licence (minimum 1 year old). An original government ID proof (Aadhaar or Passport) must be presented at pickup.

A refundable security deposit is collected before handover. It is returned within 24–48 hours of the car passing the return inspection, after adjusting any dues.

Cars are handed over with a recorded fuel level and must be returned at the same level. Fuel costs during the trip are borne by the renter; excess fuel is not reimbursed.

Each booking includes 300 km per day. Usage beyond the included limit is charged per extra kilometre at the rate confirmed on WhatsApp at the time of booking.

A grace period of 30 minutes applies after the scheduled return time. Beyond that, an hourly late fee is charged; delays over 4 hours are billed as an extra day.

All traffic fines, challans, tolls and parking charges incurred during the rental period are the renter's responsibility, including those received after the trip ends.

Any damage, loss or theft during the rental is the renter's liability up to the applicable insurance excess. Accidents must be reported to us and the police immediately. Driving under the influence voids all coverage.

The car may be driven only by the verified renter. Sub-renting, commercial use, racing, towing and off-road driving are strictly prohibited and lead to immediate termination and deposit forfeiture.

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup. Cancellations within 24 hours of pickup attract a 50% charge of one day's rent. No-shows are non-refundable.

MalayaSelfDrive operates only in Bangalore and Odisha. Interstate travel outside the permitted zone requires prior written approval on WhatsApp.
